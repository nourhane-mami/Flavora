Recipe of the Week

Try our easy Creamy Garlic Pasta, shared by one of our members.

Creamy Garlic Pasta Recipe

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 cup cooking cream
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta according to package instructions.
  2. Melt butter in a pan and sauté the garlic.
  3. Add cooking cream and stir until combined.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Add the cooked pasta and mix well.
  6. Serve with grated Parmesan cheese.
